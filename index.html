SyncText

peer-to-peer · no account
encrypted in transit · nothing stored

Paste it here, get it there.

A dead-simple way to move a bit of text between your own devices — a login code from your phone, a URL, a paragraph, an SSH key — without email, messaging apps, or a cloud account. Open this page on both devices, link them with a short code, and whatever you type on one appears live on the other. The text travels directly between the two browsers over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection; it never lands on a server.

  1. 1 Open this page on both devices.
  2. 2 Copy the code from one, Join it on the other.
  3. 3 Type or paste — it syncs live.

Won't connect? This app links your two devices with a direct "walkie-talkie" line between the browsers. Some browser add-ons — ad blockers and privacy tools especially — switch that line off. If your devices can't find each other, turn your ad blocker off for this page (usually one click on its toolbar icon), or open a private / incognito window, which turns add-ons off for you. Either way it works exactly the same.
